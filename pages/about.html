ABOUT YACO

More Than Just Interiors.

YACO Interiors & Contractor was built on a simple belief: that a beautiful home or workspace should not require a client to manage multiple contractors, chase timelines, or compromise on vision.

We bring together interior design, civil work, custom furniture and complete execution under one team. From your first conversation to the day you walk into your finished space — YACO is with you every step of the way.

Our in-house team handles design, 3D visualisation, civil work, electrical, plumbing, ceiling, painting, modular furniture and automation. When we say turnkey, we mean it.

FAQ

Common Questions.

How does the interior design process work?+

We start with a conversation to understand your vision, visit the site, prepare a mood board and initial budget, develop 2D and 3D designs, finalise the BOQ, execute the complete space and hand it over to you. Seven steps, one team.

Do you provide only design, or complete execution?+

Both. We can work on design and 3D alone, or take the entire project from concept through to turnkey execution. Most clients prefer the full journey.

Do you handle civil work?+

Yes. Our in-house team handles civil renovation, demolition, structural changes, flooring, painting, electrical and plumbing.

Do you make modular kitchens and wardrobes?+

Yes. We design and build both modular and fully custom kitchens, wardrobes, TV units, crockery units and all furniture in-house.

Can I see the 3D design before execution begins?+

Always. We present detailed 3D visualisations of every room before any execution work begins. You approve the design, then we build it.

Do you work on commercial projects?+

Yes — offices, retail, showrooms and other commercial spaces. Commercial projects follow the same complete design-to-execution process.

Simply book a consultation using the button below, or message us on WhatsApp. We'll schedule a first call and take it from there.
