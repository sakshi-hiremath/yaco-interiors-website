PROJECT

Retail Showroom — Villa Parla, Andheri West

Commercial · Retail Interior

THE SPACE

A retail showroom at Villa Parla in Andheri West, built around warm timber slat walls, glass display shelving and soft cream counters with blush fluted panels — a calm, bright backdrop for the merchandise.

THE DETAILS

A botanical wallpaper arch behind the reception counter; a textured white-brick wall lit by exposed-bulb pendants beside an arched mirror; a handcrafted false ceiling of crocheted lace hoops on jute with warm cove lighting; a green-wall entrance with rope pendant lights; and sculpted rose-gold handles.
